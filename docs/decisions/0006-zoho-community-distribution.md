# 0006 — Zoho community distribution boundaries

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-09-11

## Decision

Do not pitch Zoho CRM DevTools or other third-party products for the weekly Zoho Community Digest
or monthly Zoho Developer Community Digest. Contribute to relevant community discussions and
identify the team as the builder whenever its own resource is relevant.

Evaluate programs with explicit third-party participation separately. A proposed Developer
Hangout must teach a practical Zoho development workflow rather than serve as a product demo. A
ZUG sponsorship must follow the published sponsor process and be treated as paid promotion unless
Zoho confirms otherwise. Do not imply that participation or publication is a Zoho endorsement.

## Rationale

A Zoho Community strategist confirmed that both digests are deliberately non-promotional and
exclude third-party tools whether free, paid, or approved for Zoho Marketplace. The same response
encouraged relevant, disclosed participation in community threads.

Zoho separately publishes a Developer Hangout program that invites community presenters and a ZUG
meetup sponsorship program that expressly permits partners, Marketplace developers, and consultants
to represent products or services. Those programs have different purposes and selection criteria,
so the digest boundary should not be generalized beyond them.

## Consequences

- Digest pitches and requests for digest-editor contacts stop here.
- Community posts must solve the thread's problem and disclose that ZSetup built the linked tool.
- Any Developer Hangout proposal leads with education: auditing and maintaining Deluge functions.
- ZUG sponsorship is evaluated on audience fit, price, and attributed installs before committing.
- App Spotlight, Marketplace placement, and Zoho One's browser-extension catalog remain unconfirmed
  possibilities; verify current eligibility with the owning team before proposing them.
