✪ Editor Themes
Choose from a selection of handpicked themes to give the editor a cleaner, more modern look.

✪ Code Enhancements
Improve readability with indent guides and enhanced syntax highlighting for Deluge.

✪ Search All Functions
Search across every Deluge function in your org at once — names, API names, descriptions, and source code — with a syntax-highlighted source preview, then jump straight into Zoho's native editor to make the change.

✪ Search All Workflows
Search every workflow rule in your org at once — criteria, actions, field updates, email templates and linked Deluge functions — right from Setup › Workflow Rules. Included with Plus, Pro and Max.

✪ Export Functions
Download every Deluge function in your org as JSON, CSV or Markdown, straight from Setup › Developer Hub › Functions. Files are saved locally on your device. Included with Plus, Pro and Max.

✪ Privacy
Your Zoho data stays in your browser. Search All Workflows reads your org's configuration directly from Zoho and caches it on your device, and exports are saved as local files. Your Zoho data and function code are never sent to our servers or to Stripe.

Sign-in is optional and opens in a normal browser tab. Account tokens are stored locally on your device.

Payments are processed by Stripe on devtools.zsetup.com, outside the Chrome Web Store. The seller is Roman Shostak LLC (ZSetup), not Google. You can manage or cancel your subscription on our website.
